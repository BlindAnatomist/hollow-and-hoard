# Hollow & Hoard

Hollow & Hoard is an independent nocturnal creature-merging game built around tactile movement, transformation, discovery, and the gradual awakening of a hidden hollow.

The player brings matching creatures together, develops distinct evolutionary lines, uncovers relics, and moves completed treasures into a permanent Hoard Chamber. Successful completion should expand the world rather than clog the active board.

## Current state

The repository is in its project-definition and foundation stage. No application framework, workflow, deployment target, or production implementation has yet been accepted.

The initial concept includes:

- a moonlit hollow with handcrafted storybook atmosphere;
- goblin and gargoyle creature families;
- drag-based merging as the primary interaction;
- a discovery codex for living creatures;
- a separate Hoard Chamber for completed relics;
- environmental change as a visible and nonvisual record of progress;
- gentle, materially grounded sound without painful high-frequency effects;
- accessibility and iPhone VoiceOver behavior as functional requirements from the first playable build.

## Project separation

Hollow & Hoard may inherit proven interaction, accessibility, testing, and repository-management lessons from Moticos for Cynthia, but it is a separate game. Do not import Moticos code, assets, naming, progression, or visual identity unless a bounded assignment explicitly authorizes a specific transfer.

## Cost boundary

This project operates under a strict zero-dollar policy. No paid service, billable runner, paid asset, subscription, infrastructure charge, or other expenditure may be introduced without the owner's explicit authorization.
