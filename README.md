# Hollow & Hoard

Hollow & Hoard is an independent nocturnal creature-merging game for Cynthia, built around tactile dragging, transformation, discovery, and the gradual awakening of a hidden hollow.

The player brings matching creatures together, develops distinct evolutionary lines, creates relics, and moves completed treasures into a permanent Hoard Chamber. Successful completion expands the world rather than clogging the active board.

## Current state

The repository is in art-direction and first-playable preparation.

Accepted direction includes:

- a five-by-five carved-stone board;
- the Goblin Hollowkin family as the first playable line;
- drag-based relocation and merging;
- a separate Spawner, Codex, and Hoard Chamber;
- handcrafted nocturnal storybook art;
- warm lantern light against cool moonlight;
- gentle, materially grounded sound;
- no currency, monetization, turn limit, or passive waiting loop.

No application framework, workflow, deployment target, or hosted build has yet been accepted.

## Intended player

This game is for Cynthia.

Her experience of beauty, movement, sound comfort, pacing, and delight is the principal acceptance standard. VoiceOver-specific game controls are not part of the first-playable scope.

The blind owner remains project director. All visual work must therefore be inspected and described concretely rather than left as unexplained image output.

## Project separation

Hollow & Hoard may inherit proven interaction, testing, and repository-management lessons from Moticos for Cynthia, but it is a separate game. Do not import Moticos code, assets, naming, progression, or visual identity unless a bounded assignment explicitly authorizes a specific transfer.

## Cost boundary

This project operates under a strict zero-dollar policy. No paid service, billable runner, paid asset, subscription, infrastructure charge, or other expenditure may be introduced without the owner's explicit authorization.
